#   miniexchange --script scripts/demo.txt
#
# 1. Build an order book for AAPL: three sellers, two buyers, no overlap yet.
sell AAPL 100 150.50 bob
sell AAPL 50 150.25 carol
sell AAPL 70 150.25 dave
buy  AAPL 80 149.90 erin
buy  AAPL 40 149.75 frank
book AAPL

# 2. Price-time priority: alice's buy sweeps the best price (150.25) first,
#    carol before dave because carol arrived first, then 10 at 150.50.
#    Every fill executes at the resting order's price.
buy AAPL 130 150.50 alice
book AAPL

# 3. Partial fill: grace sells 100 at 149.80; only erin's 80 crosses.
#    The unfilled 20 rests on the ask side.
sell AAPL 100 149.80 grace
quote AAPL

# 4. Market and time-in-force orders.
#    heidi's market buy takes grace's 20 @ 149.80 and bob's 90 @ 150.50;
#    the other 40 is cancelled because no asks are left.
buy  AAPL 150 MKT heidi
#    ivan's fill-or-kill needs 50, but only frank's 40 bid is >= 149.70: killed, no trade.
sell AAPL 50 149.70 FOK ivan
#    judy's immediate-or-cancel sells 25 to frank; nothing rests.
sell AAPL 25 149.70 IOC judy

# 5. Cancel frank's remaining 15 (order #5).
orders AAPL
cancel 5
book AAPL

# 6. Transaction records and statistics.
trades AAPL
stats
metrics
